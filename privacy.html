VRG Social – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

"VRG Social" is an internal publishing tool operated by VR Gurukul (Vishvambhardaas Rishi Gurukul Sikhsha Samiti), Kaniwara Road, Sankarna, Jalore, Rajasthan 343001, India. It is used only by the school's own staff to publish the school's own pre-approved posts and short videos to the school's official accounts (Instagram @vrg_jalor, the "VR Gurukul" Facebook Page and the "VR Gurukul" YouTube channel @vrgurukuljalore).

What data the tool accesses

  • YouTube (Google) data: the tool uses the YouTube Data API only to upload the school's own videos to the school's own channel and to read that channel's basic information (channel name and ID) to confirm it is connected. It does not read, store or share data about viewers, subscribers or any other users.
  • Instagram / Facebook data: the tool publishes the school's own posts to the school's own accounts and reads the account's own recent post captions to avoid posting duplicates.

How data is stored

Access credentials are stored only on the school's own computer and in the school's private, access-controlled repository settings. No personal data of the public is collected, sold, shared or used for advertising.

Children's privacy

Photos or videos of students are published only with written parental consent, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. To request removal of any content, contact the school; content is removed within 72 hours.

Revoking access

The account owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in Instagram/Facebook settings under "Apps and websites". Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
